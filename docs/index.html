Wordocs

Word-style numbered captions, lists of tables and figures, and cross-references for Google Docs.

Source on GitHub Privacy policy Support

What it does

Wordocs brings the caption and cross-reference tools people miss from Microsoft Word to Google Docs. It is built for theses, reports and papers where tables and figures must be numbered and referenced correctly.

Captions
  • Add numbered table and figure captions from a sidebar
  • Captions renumber automatically when you open the document or insert a new one
  • Put table captions above (default) or below the table
Lists of tables and figures
  • Insert a List of Tables or List of Figures; every entry links to its caption
  • Update all lists with one click
Cross-references
  • Insert clickable references such as "Table 2", "2", or the full caption text
  • References stay correct when captions are renumbered
Refresh All and Settings
  • One click renumbers captions, updates references and rebuilds lists
  • Use your own labels, such as "Tab.", "Tabelle" or "Exhibit"

Permissions, in plain words

  • View and manage documents this add-on is installed in: Wordocs reads and edits captions, lists and references in the document you have open, and nothing else.
  • Display content in sidebars and dialogs: needed to show the Wordocs menu and sidebars.

Wordocs does not send your content anywhere and has no tracking. See the privacy policy.
