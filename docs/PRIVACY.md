# Wordocs Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-11-09_

Wordocs is a Google Docs add-on that adds Word-style table and figure captions, lists of tables and figures, and cross-references.

## What Wordocs accesses

- **The content of the Google Doc you are using it in.** It reads caption paragraphs, bookmarks and links, and edits them when you insert a caption, a list or a cross-reference, or when it renumbers captions. It uses the `documents.currentonly` permission, which only covers the document that is open.
- It does **not** access your other documents, your Drive files, your email, your contacts or your calendar.

## What Wordocs stores

Wordocs stores a few settings (caption labels, table caption position and caption formatting defaults) in the document's own script properties, using Google's Apps Script `PropertiesService`. These settings live inside the document and are removed with it.

## What Wordocs does not do

- It does not send document content or any other data to the developer or to any third-party server.
- It does not use analytics, tracking, advertising or cookies of its own.
- It does not sell or share data.

All processing happens inside Google's Apps Script environment, under Google's own terms and privacy policy.
