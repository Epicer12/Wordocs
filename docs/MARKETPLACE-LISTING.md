## Short description

Word-style numbered captions, lists of tables and figures, and cross-references for Google Docs.

## Detailed description

Wordocs brings the caption and cross-reference tools people miss from Microsoft Word to Google Docs. It is built for theses, reports and papers where tables and figures must be numbered and referenced correctly.

**Captions**
- Add numbered table and figure captions from a sidebar (Wordocs > Caption)
- Captions renumber automatically when you open the document or insert a new one
- Place table captions above (the default) or below the table
- Format captions from the sidebar and apply the style to all captions at once

**Lists of tables and figures**
- Insert a List of Tables or List of Figures at your cursor
- Every entry links to its caption
- Update all lists with one click

**Cross-references**
- Insert clickable references such as "Table 2", "2" or the full caption text
- References keep pointing at the right caption and update when captions are renumbered

**Refresh All**
- One click (Wordocs > Refresh All) renumbers captions, updates cross-references and rebuilds lists

**Settings**
- Use your own labels, for example "Tab.", "Tabelle", "Fig." or "Exhibit"; existing captions, lists and references are renamed for you

**Privacy**
Wordocs only works on the document you have open, using the narrow "current document only" permission. It does not send your content anywhere and has no tracking.

Open source (MIT): https://github.com/Epicer12/Wordocs
